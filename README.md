> **SKILLRISE AI** is a premium, futuristic, cinematic 3D web application designed to empower students, fresh graduates, and unemployed youth to identify their skill gaps, understand current industry requirements, discover matching regional job opportunities, and become employment-ready through personalized AI learning roadmaps.

---

## 🌅 Design Vision & Sunrise Color Palette

The aesthetic is inspired by a **glorious sunrise in a dark sky**, where orange, red, soft white, electric purple, and neon pink light blend seamlessly:

- **Deep Black**: `#080711` (Primary background, deep space feel)
- **Midnight Purple**: `#160B2A` (Glassmorphic cards & atmospheric depth)
- **Electric Purple**: `#8B35E8` (Atmospheric glow & accents)
- **Sunset Orange**: `#FF7417` (Sunrise energy, motivation & focus)
- **Sunrise Red**: `#F04458` (Transformation & momentum)
- **Neon Pink**: `#F044A5` (Vibrant highlights & indicators)
- **Soft Sunrise White**: `#FFF0E8` (Headings & crisp readability)

## 🔮 3D Character Transformation Story (Hero Section)

The right side of the landing page features a real-time **Three.js WebGL 3D Transformation Scene** depicting a student/job seeker undergoing a 3-stage metamorphosis:

1. **Stage 1 – Limited Knowledge**:
   - The young man begins in a dark, uncertain environment.
   - Surrounded by faded educational symbols, floating books, basic code brackets `< />`, and unlit skill indicators.
   - Lit by subtle midnight purple and deep crimson rim lighting.
2. **Stage 2 – Learning & Growth**:
   - Stepping forward through a glowing sunrise energy portal.
   - Floating 3D programming icons (Python, React, AI, Cloud), certificates, and swirling code sparks.
   - Enveloped in sunrise orange, neon pink, and electric purple light.
3. **Stage 3 – Career-Ready Professional**:
   - The same young man stands upright and confident in a sharp tech blazer against a cinematic sunrise horizon.
   - Holding an illuminated futuristic 3D laptop projecting a holographic career dashboard, accompanied by golden achievement shields and verified skill badges.
   - Bathed in radiant sunrise white, orange, and electric purple lighting.

Users can interactively switch stages, orbit the 3D camera in 360°, and trigger the **"Auto Morph Story"** mode.

## 🚀 Key Modules & Pages

### 1. Split-Screen Authentication (Sign In & Create Account)
- Left side: 3D sunrise backdrop, floating tech badges, inspiring mission statement.
- Right side: Dark glassmorphic card with social authentication (Google, GitHub), email/password login, demo accounts for instant testing, and seamless registration.

### 2. User Dashboard
- **Overview**: Real-time KPI tiles tracking Total Skills, Match %, Missing Gaps, Course Assignments, Roadmap Progress, and Suitable Openings.
- **My Resume**: 3D drag-and-drop resume uploader with glowing AI laser scanning animation, extracted technical skills, soft skills, and 4 preset demo profiles.
- **Skill Gap Analysis**: Multi-axis 3D spider radar chart, circular match gauge, and 4-column matrix classifying Matched Skills, Critical Missing Gaps, Recommended Additions, and Bonus Differentiators.
- **Job Market Insights**: Top demanded skills ranking with YoY growth stats (Gen AI +65%, Solar PV +42%), sector opportunity donut, and regional filters.
- **AI Learning Roadmap**: Milestone pathway mapped to 100% free courses (Harvard CS50, freeCodeCamp, Microsoft Learn, Kaggle, UN CC:Learn), featuring interactive completion checkboxes that dynamically boost your Employability Score in real time.
- **Recommended Jobs**: Matching regional vacancies with match percentage badges and 1-click application submissions.
